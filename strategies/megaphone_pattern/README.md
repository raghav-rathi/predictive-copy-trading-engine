# Megaphone Pattern

Broadening formation / megaphone (Edwards & Magee): a pattern of 3 successive
higher highs AND 3 successive lower lows, alternating, with each swing
expanding on the previous by at least 0.5x ATR(14) — diverging boundaries and
an expanding range. Swing pivots are 5-bar local extrema, only usable 2 bars
after the extreme bar.

## Rules

- Pattern: 3 higher highs and 3 lower lows, alternating (H-L-H-L-H-L or
  L-H-L-H-L-H), each swing larger than the previous by >= 0.5x ATR(14).
- Long: after the 3rd lower low is in, close above the most recent swing high.
- Short: after the 3rd higher high is in, close below the most recent swing low.
- Exit long: close below the pattern midline (midpoint of the last swing high
  and last swing low). Exit short is the mirror.
- Only the most recent completed pattern counts; a pattern is consumed once it
  triggers an entry, and a new pattern must complete before re-arming.
