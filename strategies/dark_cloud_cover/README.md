# Dark Cloud Cover

Dark cloud cover: after an uptrend, a strong bullish bar is followed by a bar that gaps above its high but closes below its midpoint — a failed breakout and a short signal. The mirror (piercing pattern) after a downtrend signals a long.

## Rules

- Uptrend: bar 1 closes above SMA(50) (short setup) / below SMA(50) (long setup).
- Short: bar 1 bullish (close > open) with a body ≥ 0.6× its range; bar 2 opens above bar 1's high and closes below bar 1's midpoint ((open₁ + close₁)/2) → enter on bar 2's close. Exit on a close above bar 2's high.
- Long: bar 1 bearish with a body ≥ 0.6× its range; bar 2 opens below bar 1's low and closes above bar 1's midpoint → enter on bar 2's close. Exit on a close below bar 2's low.
- Signals on bar i use only data through bar i's close. ATR stop / trail from the risk layer also applies.
