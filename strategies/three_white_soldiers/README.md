# Three White Soldiers

Three white soldiers: three consecutive bullish candles after a downtrend, each closing higher, opening inside the previous candle's body, with a small upper wick and a body of at least 0.5× ATR(14). The mirror pattern (three black crows) after an uptrend signals a short.

## Rules

- Prior trend: close below SMA(50) for each of the 5 bars before the pattern (long setup) / above SMA(50) (short setup).
- Long: 3 consecutive bullish candles (close > open), each close above the previous close, each open inside the previous candle's body, each upper wick ≤ 25% of its range, each body ≥ 0.5× ATR(14) → enter on the close of the 3rd candle. Exit on a close below the 1st soldier's open.
- Short: mirror with three black crows (each close below the previous close, lower wick ≤ 25% of range, body ≥ 0.5× ATR(14)) → enter on the close of the 3rd crow. Exit on a close above the 1st crow's open.
- Signals on bar i use only data through bar i's close. ATR stop / trail from the risk layer also applies.
