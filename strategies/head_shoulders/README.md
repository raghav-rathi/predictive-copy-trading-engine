# Head and Shoulders

The textbook reversal: three peaks with the middle (head) highest
and the two shoulders lower and roughly equal (within 3%), or the
mirror for the inverse pattern. The neckline connects the two
troughs (tops) or the two peaks (inverse); the conservative trigger
used here is the lower trough for tops and the higher peak for
bottoms. The pattern is pending once the right shoulder confirms
(5-bar zigzag lag), and the trade is the neckline break.

## Rules

- H&S top pending: head higher than both shoulders, shoulders
  within 3%. Short on a close below the neckline (lower trough).
- Inverse H&S pending: head lower than both shoulders, shoulders
  within 3%. Long on a close above the neckline (higher peak).
- Invalidation: price exceeds the head.
- Exit: close back through the entry neckline (reclaim), or the
  opposite pattern's break.
- Risk: 2xATR stop, 2.5xATR chandelier trail, no fixed target,
  120-bar (5-day) max hold, 1% equity risk per trade, 3x leverage cap

Source: head and shoulders (Bulkowski, Encyclopedia of Chart
Patterns).
