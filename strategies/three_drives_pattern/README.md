# Three Drives Pattern

Three Drives harmonic pattern on a zigzag pivot sequence. Bullish: three successive lower pivot lows D1, D2, D3 separated by pullback highs; each pullback retraces 0.618 or 0.786 (±0.08) of the prior drive and each new drive extends 1.272 or 1.618 (±0.10) of the prior pullback; drive lengths in bars must be symmetric (each within 0.5x–2x of the first). Enter long on reversal confirmation (close > open and close > previous close) on the first bar after D3 completes, within 10 bars. Exit long at D3 + 0.618 × (drive-start high − D3). Bearish is the mirror image.

## Rules

- Long: bullish Three Drives completed (D3 confirmed, 2 bars after its extreme bar) and the first reversal bar after completion.
- Short: bearish Three Drives completed and the first reversal bar after completion.
- Exit: close reaches the 61.8% retracement of the full move (plus ATR stop / trail).
- Pivots are 5-bar local extrema with a minimum 0.9x ATR(14) swing, usable only 2 bars after the extreme bar — no lookahead.
