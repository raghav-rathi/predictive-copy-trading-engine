# Episodic Pivot

Episodic Pivot (Pradeep Bonde / Qullamaggie), adapted from daily equities to 1h crypto: after a dull base (ATR at/below its 50-bar median), a bar gaps >=2% above the prior close on >=3x average volume and closes above the prior 20-bar high. Long only; the gap bar's continuation is the trade, exit on a close back below SMA(20).

## Rules

- Long: dull base, then a bar gaps >=2% on >=3x volume and closes above the prior 20-bar high.
- No short leg (the setup is canonically long-only).
- Exit: close below SMA(20) (plus ATR stop / trail).
