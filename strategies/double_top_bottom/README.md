# Double Top / Double Bottom

The classic reversal: two swing highs within 1.5% of each other
separated by a swing low (the neckline), or the mirror for double
bottoms. Built on 5-bar zigzag pivots with the same causal
confirmation as abcd_pattern. The pattern is pending once the second
extreme confirms, and the trade is the neckline break — not the
pattern completion itself, since the break often precedes
confirmation.

## Rules

- Double top pending: two swing highs within 1.5%, neckline = the
  intervening swing low. Short on a close below the neckline.
- Double bottom pending: two swing lows within 1.5%, neckline = the
  intervening swing high. Long on a close above the neckline.
- Invalidation: price exceeds the second extreme (the pattern is
  dead, not just untriggered).
- Exit: close back through the entry neckline (reclaim), or the
  opposite pattern's break.
- Risk: 2xATR stop, 2.5xATR chandelier trail, no fixed target,
  96-bar (4-day) max hold, 1% equity risk per trade, 3x leverage cap

Source: double top / double bottom (Bulkowski, Encyclopedia of
Chart Patterns).
