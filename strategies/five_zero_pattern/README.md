# 5-0 Pattern

Scott Carney's 5-0 harmonic pattern (Harmonic Trading Vol. 2, 2007). Bullish: 0=low, X=high, A=low, B=high with AB = 1.13–1.618× XA (±0.10), C=low with BC = 1.618–2.24× AB (±0.10); B must exceed X and C must fall below A. D is the 50% retracement of BC (±0.08), reached after C. Enter long at D on reversal confirmation (close > open and close > previous close). Exit long at the 38.2% retracement of the BC leg measured from B (C + 0.618×BC), above the D entry. Bearish is the mirror image.

## Rules

- Long: bullish 5-0 completed (C confirmed, 2 bars after its extreme bar) and the close reaches the D level with reversal confirmation, within 20 bars.
- Short: bearish 5-0 completed and the close reaches the D level with reversal confirmation, within 20 bars.
- Exit: close reaches the 38.2% retracement of the BC leg measured from B (C + 0.618×BC for longs; the mirror for shorts), plus ATR stop / trail. Spec correction: the original brief specified the exit as the 38.2% retracement of the C-to-D leg measured from C, which sits below the D entry price and exits almost immediately at a small loss; corrected to the standard BC-leg retracement target before shipping (see CALIBRATION.md).
- Pivots are 5-bar local extrema with a minimum 0.9x ATR(14) swing, usable only 2 bars after the extreme bar — no lookahead.
