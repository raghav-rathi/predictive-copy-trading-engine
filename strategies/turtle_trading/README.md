# Turtle Trading

The Original Turtle Trading Rules (Curtis Faith): System 1 longs on a close above the prior 20-bar high / shorts below the prior 20-bar low, exiting on a close through the opposite 10-bar channel; System 2 uses a 55-bar entry / 20-bar exit. Either system firing enters. N = ATR(20).

## Rules

- Long: close above prior 20-bar highest high (System 1) or prior 55-bar highest high (System 2).
- Short: close below prior 20-bar lowest low (System 1) or prior 55-bar lowest low (System 2).
- Exit: close back through the opposite 10-bar channel (plus ATR stop / trail).
- Channels are compared to the prior bar's values (shifted by 1) — a close can never exceed its own bar's channel.
