# Wolfe Wave

Bill Wolfe's Wolfe Wave: a 5-pivot zigzag sequence. Bullish: low1, high2, lower low3, lower high4, low5 breaking below the 1-3 trendline, with the 1-3 and 2-4 lines converging (intersecting ahead in time). Enter long on the close back above the 1-3 trendline within 20 bars of point 5 completing; exit long when the close reaches the EPA line (through points 1 and 4). Bearish is the mirror image.

## Rules

- Long: bullish Wolfe Wave completed (point 5 confirmed, 2 bars after its extreme bar) and the close crosses back above the 1-3 trendline within 20 bars.
- Short: bearish Wolfe Wave completed and the close crosses back below the 1-3 trendline within 20 bars.
- Exit: close reaches the EPA line (plus ATR stop / trail).
- Pivots are 5-bar local extrema with a minimum 0.9x ATR(14) swing, usable only 2 bars after the extreme bar — no lookahead.
