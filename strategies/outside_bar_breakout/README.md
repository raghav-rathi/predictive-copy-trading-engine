# Outside Bar Breakout

Outside (engulfing-range) bar breakout: a bar whose high exceeds the prior bar's high AND whose low undercuts the prior bar's low, closing in the top (bullish) or bottom (bearish) third of its range. Entry on the next bar's close beyond the outside bar's extreme; exit on an opposite outside bar.

## Rules

- Long: bullish outside bar (close in top third), next close above its high.
- Short: bearish outside bar (close in bottom third), next close below its low.
- Exit: opposite outside bar (plus ATR stop / trail).
