# Bollinger Bandwidth Breakout

Bollinger Bandwidth (20,2): long on a close above the upper band while bandwidth is expanding above its 20-bar average; short on a close below the lower band while expanding; exit at the mid-band.

Source: John Bollinger Bandwidth / %B family (Bollinger on Bollinger Bands) — https://www.investopedia.com/terms/b/bollingerbands.asp

Paper only: historical/backtest signals, no live execution.
