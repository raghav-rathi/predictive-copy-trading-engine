# Rising Three Methods

Rising/falling three methods continuation pattern on 1h candles. A long bar-1 candle in the trend direction, three small counter-trend candles fully inside bar 1's range, then a bar-5 candle closing beyond bar 1's extreme.

## Rules

- Rising three methods: uptrend (close above SMA(50)); bar 1 long bullish candle, body ≥ 1.0 × ATR(14); bars 2–4 small bearish candles (close < open), each fully inside bar 1's range (high < bar 1 high, low > bar 1 low); bar 5 bullish closing above bar 1's high → long entry on bar 5's close. Exit on a close below bar 1's low.
- Falling three methods: mirror image — downtrend (close below SMA(50)); bar 1 long bearish candle; bars 2–4 small bullish candles inside bar 1's range; bar 5 bearish closing below bar 1's low → short entry. Exit on a close above bar 1's high.
- Bar 1's extremes are forward-filled from the pattern bar so the exit rule can reference them. ATR stop / trail from the risk layer also applies.
