# Stochastic + MACD Combo

Stochastic(14,3) + MACD(12,26,9) confirmation combo: long on a %K cross above %D while %K is under 30 (oversold) and MACD sits above its signal line; short on a %K cross below %D while %K is over 70 and MACD sits below its signal line; exit on the opposite %K/%D cross.

## Rules

- Long: %K crosses above %D with %K < 30 and MACD > signal.
- Short: %K crosses below %D with %K > 70 and MACD < signal.
- Exit: opposite %K/%D cross (plus ATR stop / trail).
