# Morning/Evening Star

Steve Nison's Morning/Evening Star 3-candle reversal pattern. Morning star (completes on bar i): candle1 (i−2) long red body (≥ 0.6× ATR(14)); candle2 (i−1) small body (≤ 0.3× candle1's body); candle3 (i) long green body closing beyond the midpoint of candle1's body; trend filter close below SMA(50). Evening star is the mirror image, with close above SMA(50).

## Rules

- Long: completed morning star → enter (harness executes at the next bar's open).
- Short: completed evening star → enter.
- Exit: the opposite star pattern (long exits on an evening star, short on a morning star), plus the ATR stop / trail from the risk layer.
- No gap requirement: crypto trades 24/7, so the textbook gap between candles does not apply. All quantities use data through bar i's close only — no lookahead.
