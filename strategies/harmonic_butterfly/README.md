# Harmonic Butterfly

Harmonic Butterfly pattern (Scott Carney): five swing points X-A-B-C-D whose leg ratios fall in the Butterfly bands (B 0.736-0.836 of XA, C 0.332-0.936 of AB, D 1.222-1.668 of XA). Entry at D completion (reversal), exit on the opposite pattern plus ATR stop / trail.

## Rules

- Long: bullish Butterfly completes at D (XA up, ratios in band).
- Short: bearish Butterfly completes at D (XA down, ratios in band).
- Exit: opposite pattern completion (plus ATR stop / trail).
