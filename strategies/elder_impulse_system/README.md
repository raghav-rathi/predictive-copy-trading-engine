# Elder Impulse System

Elder Impulse System (Alexander Elder, "Come Into My Trading Room", 2002): green bar = EMA(13) rising AND MACD histogram (12,26,9) rising; red bar = both falling; blue bar = disagreement between the two. Long on the first green bar after a red/blue bar; short on the first red bar after a green/blue bar. Exit long on a red or blue bar; exit short on a green or blue bar.

## Rules

- Long: first green impulse bar after a red or blue bar.
- Short: first red impulse bar after a green or blue bar.
- Exit long: red or blue bar. Exit short: green or blue bar (plus ATR stop / trail).
