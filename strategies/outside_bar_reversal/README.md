# Outside-Bar Reversal

An outside bar engulfs the previous bar (high above the previous high, low below the previous low). Reversals at swing extremes: after a downtrend, an outside bar closing in the upper third of its range on above-average volume is a bullish reversal; the mirror after an uptrend is a bearish reversal.

## Rules

- Prior trend: close below SMA(20) on the bar before the outside bar (bullish setup) / above SMA(20) (bearish setup).
- Long: outside bar closes in the upper third of its range (close ≥ low + ⅔·range) with volume above its 20-bar average → enter. Exit on a bearish outside bar (outside bar closing in its lower third) or on a close below the low of the most recent outside bar.
- Short: outside bar closes in the lower third of its range (close ≤ low + ⅓·range) with volume above its 20-bar average → enter. Exit on a bullish outside bar or on a close above the high of the most recent outside bar.
- Signals on bar i use only data through bar i's close. ATR stop / trail from the risk layer also applies.
