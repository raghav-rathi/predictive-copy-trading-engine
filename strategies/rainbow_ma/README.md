# Rainbow Moving Average

Rainbow MA: 22 EMAs from 9 to 100 periods. Long when price crosses above the whole ribbon (above every EMA) while the fastest EMA is rising; short when price crosses below the whole ribbon while the fastest EMA is falling; exit when price falls back inside the ribbon.

## Rules

- Long: close crosses above the highest of the 22 EMAs, fastest EMA rising.
- Short: close crosses below the lowest of the 22 EMAs, fastest EMA falling.
- Exit: close back inside the ribbon (below fastest EMA for longs).
