# Donchian Channel

Donchian 20-bar breakout: long on a close above the prior 20-bar highest high, short on a close below the prior 20-bar lowest low; exit on a close back through the channel midline.

## Rules

- Long: close above prior 20-bar highest high.
- Short: close below prior 20-bar lowest low.
- Exit: close back through the channel midline (plus ATR stop / trail).
- The close is compared to the prior bar's channel values (shifted by 1) — a close can never exceed its own bar's channel.
