# Harmonic Shark

Harmonic Shark pattern (Scott Carney): five swing points X-A-B-C-D whose leg ratios fall in the Shark bands (B 0.332-0.668 of XA, C 1.08-1.668 of AB, D 0.836-1.18 of XA). Entry at D completion (reversal), exit on the opposite pattern plus ATR stop / trail.

## Rules

- Long: bullish Shark completes at D (XA up, ratios in band).
- Short: bearish Shark completes at D (XA down, ratios in band).
- Exit: opposite pattern completion (plus ATR stop / trail).
