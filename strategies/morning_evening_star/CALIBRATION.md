# Morning Star / Evening Star — Calibration

Big body >1% and star body <0.3% (1h quantifications of Nison's qualitative sizes); gap + midpoint-close confirmation required.

All parameters fixed a priori from the source spec. No in-sample sweep, nothing selected on the backtest window.

Adaptation (material): 24/7 crypto has no overnight gaps, so the textbook gap is replaced by the star opening beyond the prior close (below it for morning, above it for evening). Without this the pattern can never fire on 1h crypto — verified: the strict-gap version produced 0 trades on all three coins.
