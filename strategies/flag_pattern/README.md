# Flag Pattern Breakout

The textbook flag: a near-vertical pole, then a short, tight
consolidation, then a breakout in the pole's direction. The pole
must move at least 3x ATR(14) within 12 bars AND stall (the last two
bars nearly flat) — the stall requirement is load-bearing: without
it, a steady trend triggers "poles" mid-move and the consolidation
logic never gets a clean window. The consolidation must last 5–15
bars, span at most 1.5x ATR, and retrace at most 60% of the pole.

## Rules

- Bull flag armed: pole up (>=3xATR in <=12 bars, stalled) +
  consolidation (5–15 bars, <=1.5xATR range, <=60% retrace). Long on
  a close above the consolidation high.
- Bear flag armed: the mirror. Short on a close below the
  consolidation low.
- Invalidation: price breaks the consolidation's far side by more
  than 0.5x ATR.
- Exit: close back through the entry level (failed breakout), or
  the opposite flag's breakout.
- Risk: 1.5xATR stop, 2xATR trail, no fixed target, 72-bar (3-day)
  max hold, 1% equity risk per trade, 3x leverage cap

Source: flag pattern (Bulkowski, Encyclopedia of Chart Patterns).
