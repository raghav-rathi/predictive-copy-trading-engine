# Fibonacci Pullback Entry

Buy the pullback, not the breakout: in an SMA200 uptrend, enter long
when price dips into the 50–61.8% retracement pocket of the trailing
60-bar swing leg but holds above 78.6% and reclaims 61.8% by the close.
Shorts mirror on the 38.2–50% bounce zone in downtrends.

**Exits** — long at the prior swing high (target reached), short at the
prior swing low. The 78.6% invalidation level is approximated by the
2xATR hard stop (the harness places only ATR stops), 100-bar max hold.

Source: https://www.financialtechwiz.com/post/fibonacci-retracement-tradingview/

swing-window sensitivity sweep.
